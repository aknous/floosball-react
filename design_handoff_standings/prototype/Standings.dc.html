TURN 3

Two tiebreakers, two jobs

Division record settles the four division titles; league record orders the four wild cards. Each view shows the one that governs it — 1a carries DIV, 1b carries both with a note spelling out which applies where. 1c was a second ranked table, so it is now a track instead: the twelve teams still chasing a wild card placed along a games-back axis, the cut drawn as a rule through it, stacked where teams are level — each card carrying its record. Rank movement now appears only in the league view, where a single ordered list makes a change of position meaningful. The rules do real work: Hamburg and St. Louis take Corduroy division titles at 7–7 on 4–2 division records while 10–4 Minnesota drops to the fifth seed, and in the Flannel five teams sit level at 8–6 with identical 6–4 league records, so that cut falls through to point differential — Miami by seven points over Georgia.

1a By division Eight tables of four. Division record breaks ties.
